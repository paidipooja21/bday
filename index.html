A Letter From My Heart 💌

There are some things that I have wanted to say for a very long time.

To My Bangarm ❤️

Dear Bhanu,

I don't even know where to begin, because there is so much sitting inside my heart that I want to tell you. Maybe no letter can ever explain everything I feel... but I still want to try.

First of all... Happy Birthday, Bangarm. ❤️

I genuinely hope you are happy. I hope life gives you everything you dream about. I hope you achieve everything you want in your life. I hope you smile more, worry less, and always remember how valuable you are.

But today, I also want to tell you something that I have been carrying inside me for a very long time.

I miss you.

I miss you more than I know how to explain. I want to talk to you. I want to sit and talk with you like we used to. I want those simple conversations again. I want to hear you. I want to see you. I want to know how your day was. I want those small moments again.

You come into my mind so many times. Sometimes every second, somehow you are there in my thoughts. Chala gurthostunnav. Every second nuv naaku gurthostunnav. Something reminds me of you. A song... A place... A memory... A small thing... And suddenly, I remember you.

It has almost been one year since we really got to see each other. And honestly, I never thought I would miss someone's presence this much.

There are days when I just wish I could see you again. Even if we don't say much. Even if we just sit quietly. I think just seeing you would mean so much to me.

And Bhanu... if I have ever hurt you, if I have ever said something wrong, if I have ever done something that made you feel bad, emaina chesi unte, I am really sorry.

Maybe I don't always know how to express myself properly. Maybe sometimes my emotions come out in the wrong way. But I never wanted to intentionally hurt you. For anything I have done that caused you pain, I am sorry, Bangarm.

And even after everything that happened... I never really felt hatred towards you. Honestly, I never hated you.

I don't know how to hate you. Because the person who became so important to my heart cannot simply become a stranger inside my memories.

From the very beginning, the way I liked you... I still like you the same way.

Time passed. Things happened. We changed. We had misunderstandings. We had difficult moments. But my feelings didn't suddenly disappear because of them.

EM JARIGINA... I still care about you. I still like you. I still love you.

Even if someone says something bad about you, I don't simply believe it. Because I trust you deeply.

Nenu ninu chala nammutunna... chala ante chala.

Nuv ala cheyav ani na heart lo oka nammakam undi. I don't know how to explain it, but I trust the person I knew and cared for.

I don't know what you think about me today. I don't know what is in your heart. And I don't want to force an answer from you. I just want you to know what is in mine.

I love you, Bhanu.

And I still have the same feelings for you. I have the same feelings for u.

You are still one of the most special people in my life. Your place in my life is something I don't want to replace.

I don't want to replace your place in my life with someone else. Because your place became yours through everything we experienced together.

Every special moment between us is still somewhere in my memory. I remember our conversations. I remember our happiness. I remember our silly moments. I remember the little things. I remember how we used to talk to each other.

Sometimes I feel your presence even now. You are not physically standing beside me, but somehow your memories are still present in my present.

Sometimes I even dream about meeting you. In my dreams, we talk again. We laugh again. Everything feels normal again. And when I wake up, for a few seconds it feels real... and then I realize it was only a dream.

And maybe the tears that come from my eyes sometimes explain something that my words cannot. They tell me how much you still mean to me.

My heart still gives your memories a value that I cannot explain.

Even now, when I think about my future, there is a part of me that hopes... maybe one day we will have those happy days again.

I keep waiting for the day when we can talk normally again. The day when our conversations don't feel distant. The day when we can laugh again. The day when we can look at each other without carrying all the pain of everything that happened.

I miss the version of us where we could simply talk to each other. I miss the happiness. I miss the comfort. I miss having you around.

And yes... I want to see you again.

More than anything, I want one chance to sit in front of you and just talk. No arguments. No pressure. No forcing anything. Just two people remembering why they once became so important to each other.

I don't know what tomorrow will bring. But I genuinely hope that someday we can find our way back to happiness.

I hope one day we can talk the way we used to. I hope one day we can laugh again. I hope one day we can make new memories instead of only remembering the old ones.

I don't want to force anything from you. I only want you to know that if someday your heart allows it, I would love the chance to talk to you again.

I would love a chance to see you again. To sit with you. To talk with you. To understand you. To listen to you. And maybe, slowly, find the happiness we once had.

Because I still believe that some beautiful things deserve another chance.

And if someday we get that chance... I would never take those moments for granted.

Bhanu... please always remember one thing.

You are special to me.

Not just today. Not just because it is your birthday. You have been special to me for a long time.

And no matter what situation life puts us in, I will always wish good things for you. I will always want to see you happy. I will always want you to achieve your dreams.

You will always have my support as someone who is very special to me. In good situations. In difficult situations. In happy days. In bad days. I will always want the best for you.

You have always been a first priority in my heart.

And I don't want to replace that place. I don't want to forget everything we had. I don't want to pretend that none of it mattered. Because it mattered. You mattered.

You still matter.

Maybe someday, when you look back at this letter, I hope you remember that there was someone who cared about you more deeply than she knew how to explain.

Someone who missed you. Someone who remembered you. Someone who still believed in the beautiful memories. Someone who still hoped that happy days could return.

I want you to remember how beautiful our happy days were. Remember the laughs. Remember the conversations. Remember the little moments. Remember the comfort. Remember the happiness.

Because those memories are still very precious to me.

I don't know if this letter can tell you everything. But if there is one thing I want you to understand, it is this:

I miss you.

I care about you.

I trust you.

I still love you.

I still have the same feelings.

And I still hope that someday we can smile together again. ❤️

If you are happy, please remember me sometimes. I will always be happy to know that you are doing well.

If you achieve your dreams, I will be proud of you. If life becomes difficult, I hope you remember that there is someone who genuinely wishes good things for you.

And if someday you remember me... I hope you remember me with a smile.

Remember the girl who once loved talking to you. Remember the girl who kept your memories close to her heart. Remember your Potti.

Because no matter how much time has passed, you are still my Bangarm.

I know life doesn't always happen the way we imagine. I know people change. I know situations change. I know sometimes two people who care about each other can still become distant. But that doesn't erase the beautiful things that once existed.

And I will always be grateful for the happiness you brought into my life.

If life gives us another chance to sit together, talk together, laugh together and make new memories... I would be grateful.

Until then... please take care of yourself. Eat properly. Sleep properly. Keep smiling. Keep chasing your dreams. And please... be happy.

Happy Birthday once again, my Bangarm.

I hope this birthday brings something beautiful into your life.

And I hope someday when you think about this birthday, you remember that someone made this little world because you were important enough to her heart to make her do it.

I miss you. More than these words can say.

I want to see you again.

I want to talk to you again.

I want us to be happy again.

And whatever happens... I will always wish good things for you.

EM JARIGINA...

You will always be someone very special to my heart. ❤️

With all my heart,

From your Potti ⭐❤️
always caring for her Bangarm
